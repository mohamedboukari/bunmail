- **Per-domain revocation of click tracking links (#424).** A click link (`/t/c/<token>`) used to be a permanent redirect that only a `DKIM_ENCRYPTION_KEY` rotation could withdraw, so a key allowed to send tracked mail could mint a lasting redirector through the operator's host. Each domain now has a tracking epoch (`domains.tracking_epoch`, migration 0045, default 0) that every new click token names and its MAC covers, next to the sender domain id; the redirect only follows a link while its domain exists and is at that epoch. Admins revoke every click link already sent from one domain with **Revoke tracking links** on the domain page (`POST /dashboard/domains/:id/tracking-revoke`, `adminGuard`, asks for confirmation) or `POST /api/v1/domains/:id/tracking-revocation` (admin keys), audited as `domain.tracking_links_revoked` with the new epoch; links in mail sent afterwards work, and deleting a domain also revokes its links. A revoked link gets the same neutral `404` as an invalid token and counts against the same rate limit, logged at `info` with ids only (never the URL); the MAC is still checked before any database access, and a failed epoch read answers `503`, never a redirect. Epochs are cached 30 s per process: the process that made the change (a revocation or a domain delete) stops the links at once, other replicas keep redirecting them for up to 30 s. A revocation does not stop the key that minted the links: revoke the key (or turn click tracking off for the domain) first. At the highest epoch a revocation is refused with `409 TRACKING_EPOCH_EXHAUSTED`. Domain responses gain `trackingEpoch`. Open pixels are not affected. Docs: `docs/tracking.md`, `docs/domains.md`, `docs/api.md`, `docs/dashboard.md`, `docs/audit-log.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `THREAT_MODEL.md` (the permanent-redirect residual risk is now mitigated).
- **Breaking for old click links (#424):** links sent before this change carry no domain, so they count as epoch 0 of their email row's domain. They keep redirecting while that email row exists and the domain was never revoked, but **a click link of an email that was deleted and purged from the trash stops redirecting** (it answers `404`), as its domain can no longer be known or revoked.
