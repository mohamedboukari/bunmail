- **MTA-STS policy for inbound mail, opt-in per domain (#472).** An admin sets a verified domain's MTA-STS mode to `testing` or `enforce` (default `none`) on its dashboard page (`POST /dashboard/domains/:id/mta-sts`, admins only) or with `PATCH /api/v1/domains/:id` and `mtaStsMode`, audited as `domain.updated`. BunMail then serves the RFC 8461 policy at `GET /.well-known/mta-sts.txt` when the request's `Host` is `mta-sts.<domain>`: `text/plain`, `Cache-Control: max-age=` the `max_age` capped at an hour, no auth, no cookie, one identical `404` for every other host, unverified domain or mode `none`; the served domains are cached 30 s per process, never a query per request. The policy lists `MTA_STS_MX` (comma list, default `MAIL_HOSTNAME`) and `MTA_STS_MAX_AGE` (86400 to 31557600 seconds; unset, one day for `testing`, one week for `enforce`); its id is a hash of the policy text, so it changes with the policy. The domain page and `dnsRecords` show the `_mta-sts.<domain>` TXT record (`v=STSv1; id=<id>`) and the `mta-sts.<domain>` host, which the reverse proxy serves with a certificate for that name. Verify also checks MTA-STS (`checks.mtaSts`, `lastCheckResult.mtaSts`): the TXT id, and a fetch of the policy the way a sender does; it never affects domain verification. Migration `0046` adds `domains.mta_sts_mode`. Receiving TLS-RPT reports for your own domains remains a follow-up.
