- **TLS-RPT reports a PKIX-only TLSA host secured by MTA-STS under the `sts` policy (#473).** With `DANE_ENABLED=true`, a session to an MX host whose TLSA records are all unusable (PKIX usages 0/1, or otherwise invalid) under an MTA-STS policy in enforce mode gets that policy's verified TLS (#452), but TLS-RPT reported it only as `tlsa-invalid` under the host's `tlsa` policy, as if no certificate had been checked. It is now recorded under the `sts` policy with the outcome of the MTA-STS verification (success, or `certificate-expired`, `certificate-host-mismatch`, `certificate-not-trusted`, `validation-failure`), and still as `tlsa-invalid` under the `tlsa` policy, since that RRset was evaluated and none of its records was found valid (RFC 8460 section 4.3.2.1; section 4 allows both policies for one domain and MX). Hosts with a usable DANE-EE or DANE-TA record stay under `tlsa` alone, and a host with no usable record and no enforce policy stays `tlsa-invalid` alone. See `docs/tls-rpt.md`.
