- **DANE-TA (usage 2) TLSA records now authenticate MX hosts (#455).** With `DANE_ENABLED=true`, a "2 x x" record used to be unusable (Bun 1.3.14 exposed only the server's leaf certificate), so a DANE-TA-only host got mandatory but unauthenticated TLS, or the MTA-STS verified TLS of an enforce policy (#452). On Bun 1.4.4 BunMail builds a certification path from the leaf to the anchor the records name, over the certificates the server presented, in any order (RFC 7672 3.1.2 and 3.2, RFC 7671 5.2): every issuer link checked (issuer name, key identifiers, keyUsage, signature), every issuer a CA, the path length constraints of the intermediates and of the anchor applied, the validity dates of every certificate below the anchor checked (not the anchor's, RFC 5280 6.1.1), and the leaf naming a reference identifier (the TLSA base domain, the recipient domain and its CNAME expansion, RFC 7672 3.2.2 and 3.2.3). A "2 0 0" or "2 1 0" record may also anchor a chain sent without it. A DANE-TA record never makes the leaf its own anchor (as in OpenSSL; DANE-EE does that). The search is bounded (10 certificates, 256 partial paths, 16 DNS-carried anchors, 64 signature verifications per check, then `chain-invalid`), so a hostile domain's records and chain cannot stall the sender. Name constraints are not applied.
  - Behaviour change for DANE-TA hosts: a session whose chain fails the check now ends before MAIL FROM and the mail stays in `retry` (failing over to the next MX host), as for DANE-EE; a DANE-TA-only host under an MTA-STS enforce policy goes back to DANE automatically. TLS reports record these sessions under the host's `tlsa` policy, as successes or as `certificate-not-trusted`, `certificate-host-mismatch`, `certificate-expired` or `validation-failure` (`dane-tlsa-mismatch`, `certificate-not-yet-valid`); no record is reported as "valid but unsupported" any more. New `debug` lines `DANE: certificate chain authenticated` (usage, anchor source and depth) and `DANE: certificate chain not authenticated` (failure).
