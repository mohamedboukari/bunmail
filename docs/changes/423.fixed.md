- **The ownership transfer burst test no longer hangs; the database pool size is configurable (#423).** `users-admin.integration.test.ts` "a parallel burst of wrong passwords runs at most the budget of verifies" hung for its whole 30 s timeout now and then (CI on PR #413, and locally in the full run). The cause is not test state but a Bun 1.3.14 bug in its PostgreSQL pool: when more queries run at once than the pool has connections, and some are statements a connection has not prepared yet, a few queries never settle (or the connection desyncs with `ERR_POSTGRES_UNSUPPORTED_INTEGER_SIZE`) while every connection is idle on the server (oven-sh/bun#32004, #33627, #34756, #35114). The burst sends 15 requests, each reading two accounts in parallel: up to 30 queries against the default pool of 10. Reproduced outside BunMail (30 concurrent queries on a pool of 10 stalls on the first round every time; on a pool of 50, 500 rounds without a stall, and a burst never stalled on a pool larger than itself) and in the test itself with a smaller pool (the same queries left pending as in the CI log). New optional `DATABASE_POOL_MAX` (1 to 1000, default 10, Bun's default; anything else refuses to boot) sizes the pool; the integration preload sets it to 32, so no test's burst reaches it (a run then holds up to 32 connections: several local runs on one Postgres must stay under its `max_connections`, see `docs/testing.md`). Production keeps 10, and the same stall can hit a busy instance: raise `DATABASE_POOL_MAX` above your peak of concurrent queries (and under Postgres's `max_connections`) until a Bun upgrade carries the upstream fixes. The test still asserts the same budget. Docs: `docs/testing.md`, `docs/self-hosting.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `.env.example`.
