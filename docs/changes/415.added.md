- **A BIMI readiness check on the domain page (#415).** A new BIMI section explains what BIMI needs and shows the `default._bimi.<domain>` record to publish; its **Check BIMI** button (admins only, `POST /dashboard/domains/:id/bimi`, sharing the Verify throttle) checks the domain and redirects with one banner per verdict: ready, not ready, no record, declined, or check failed. BunMail sends nothing differently for BIMI, and nothing is checked on page load or by Verify.
  - Record: one `v=BIMI1` record at `default._bimi.<domain>` (else the organizational domain's), read through the split-string-safe TXT lookup, with `l=` and `a=` each one https URL; an empty `l=` is a declination. DMARC: `p=quarantine` or `p=reject`, `pct=100`, and `sp=` not `none` when set.
  - Logo: fetched through the webhook SSRF guard (https only, public addresses only, pinned, no redirects, no decompression, 32 KB, 10 s) and checked against SVG Tiny PS (`version="1.2"`, `baseProfile="tiny-ps"`, a `<title>`, no `x=`/`y=`, a square `viewBox`; no script, event attributes, external references, images, links or animation), parsed with no DOCTYPE and no entity expansion, never rendered. Each failed rule is listed.
  - Certificate: `a=` is reported as present or absent only; BunMail does not validate VMC or CMC chains, and the page says Gmail and Apple Mail need one.
  - The last result is kept in memory for 30 minutes per domain (nothing in the database). ARC is not implemented: BunMail does not forward or modify received mail, so there is nothing to seal.
